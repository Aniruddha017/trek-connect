Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a trek?

Register, log in, browse the available treks, and click the Book button to reserve your slot.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Bookings can be cancelled before the trek starts from the My Bookings page.

How can I become a trek staff member?

Register as a staff directly or apply through your dashboard. Your application will be reviewed by an administrator before approval.
