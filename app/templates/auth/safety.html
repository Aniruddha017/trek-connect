Safety Guidelines

Your safety is our highest priority.
  • Wear proper trekking shoes and suitable clothing.
  • Carry sufficient drinking water.
  • Follow all instructions provided by trek staff.
  • Carry essential medications and a basic first-aid kit.
  • Respect nature and avoid littering.
  • Inform trek staff immediately if you feel unwell during the trek.
